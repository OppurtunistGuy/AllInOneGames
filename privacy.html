Privacy

Short version: the games run entirely in your browser. No account, no server, nothing about you is transmitted or kept by us.

What is stored on your device

Two values, in your browser's own local storage. They never leave your device and we cannot read them.

  • party-engine-age — whether you answered that you are 18 or over, so you are not asked again every visit.
  • party-engine-banner-dismissed — whether you closed the app banner, so it stays closed.

Clearing your browser's site data for this domain removes both. There are no cookies.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or date of birth. The age question is a single yes or no and the exact age is never asked.
  • No answers. Which prompts you saw, what you tapped and any purity score are held in memory and discarded when you close the tab.
  • No account, because there isn't one.

Hosting and third parties

The site is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. See Vercel's own privacy notice for what they retain.

Fonts are currently loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

No analytics are installed. If that changes, this page will name the tool and what it measures before it goes live.

Age

Hot mode contains explicit sexual content and is for adults only. We ask once whether you are 18 or over and take the answer at face value; we do not and cannot verify it. Answering under 18 hides Hot mode entirely and locks the Purity Test.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Data held on your own device is under your control through your browser settings.
